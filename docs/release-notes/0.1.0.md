HearOn Link 0.1.0 is the first release: an AirPods companion for Googlebooks and Android phones.

**Install:** download `HearOnLink.apk` below and open it, then follow the short setup. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
Needs Googlebook OS / Android 17 (or Android 16 QPR3 on a Pixel). No root.

- Battery for left, right and case, with charging, in the app, the Quick Settings tile and panel,
  a notification and a home-screen widget. Last known levels show how long ago each part was seen;
  the case's Bluetooth adverts fill in 1 % levels when you open it nearby.
- Listening modes (Off, Transparency, Adaptive, Noise Cancellation), press-and-hold cycle, Adaptive
  level, Noise Cancellation with one AirPod, conversation awareness, personalized volume.
- Ear detection: pause when you take an AirPod out, play again when it goes back, pause when you fall asleep.
- Stem presses: pick what press once, twice, three times and hold do, including Overview and Show desktop.
- Head gestures: a live Yes/No demo, calibration to your own head, "anytime" actions for a nod and
  a shake, and nod to answer calls on phones.
- Press speed and hold duration, volume swipe, call controls, microphone side, tone volume, case
  sounds, custom EQ (when your firmware offers it), optimized charging, rename, model and firmware.
- A Googlebook window with the AirPods on the left and settings on the right; one column on phones.
