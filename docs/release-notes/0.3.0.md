More to put on a stem press, and a better desktop window.

- **More for stem presses and head gestures:** go straight to Transparency, Adaptive, Noise
  Cancellation or Off (instead of only switching to the next mode), turn conversation awareness on
  or off, and mute or unmute. The menus list only what your AirPods have.
- **A proper desktop window:** HearOn Link now tells Android it is resizable, with a smallest size
  of a phone-sized column, and re-lays itself out while you drag instead of restarting. Plugging in
  a keyboard or mouse doesn't restart it either.

Install `HearOnLink.apk` over the previous version; your AirPods, calibration and settings stay.
