HearOn Link no longer has an accessibility service.

- **No accessibility access.** The "HearOn Link system actions" service is gone; after updating you
  won't find it in Android's accessibility settings any more.
- **Show desktop** works without it, for the AirPods you picked in Android's device picker. If you
  skipped that step in the setup, the Stem presses and Head gestures pages offer it again.
- **Open an app**: a new action for stem presses and head gestures. Pick any app from a list, for
  example press twice to open your music app, or shake your head to open the calculator.
- **Removed:** Overview, Back, Notifications, Quick Settings, Screenshot and Lock screen. Android
  only offers these to accessibility services. Presses that used them go back to "AirPods default",
  head gestures to "Nothing".

Install `HearOnLink.apk` over the previous version; your AirPods, calibration and settings stay.
