Fixes from the first day of 0.2.0.

- **Choosing your AirPods works with AirPods you've already paired.** The setup lists them and
  Android asks you to confirm the one you pick. Before, Android's list searched for AirPods in
  pairing mode and stayed empty until you closed it.
- **Show desktop and Open an app ask for that confirmation when you pick them.** The "Choose your
  AirPods first" card no longer stays on the page; a short reminder shows only if you declined.
- **Nothing disappears while the AirPods are away.** Listening mode, conversation awareness and
  personalized volume stay in place, switched off, until the AirPods connect. Right after
  connecting, the app shows what it remembers until the AirPods report their settings.
- **On a Googlebook, Back sits with the page title** at the top of the settings pane instead of the
  window's top-left corner, and Settings keeps your AirPods on the left like every other page.

Install `HearOnLink.apk` over the previous version; your AirPods, calibration and settings stay.
