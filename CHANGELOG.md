# What's new

## 0.2.0 (1 October 2026)

- **No accessibility service any more.** HearOn Link no longer asks for accessibility access at all.
- **Show desktop** now works without it, for the AirPods you picked in Android's device picker
  (the Stem presses and Head gestures pages offer the picker if you skipped it in the setup).
- **Open an app**: a new action for stem presses and head gestures. Pick any app from a list.
- Gone, because Android only offers them to accessibility services: Overview, Back, Notifications,
  Quick Settings, Screenshot and Lock screen. Presses that used them go back to "AirPods default",
  head gestures to "Nothing".

## 0.1.0 (30 September 2026)

The first release: an AirPods companion for Googlebooks and Android phones.

- Battery for left, right and case, with charging, in the app, the Quick Settings tile and panel,
  a notification and a home-screen widget. Last known levels show how long ago each part was seen;
  the case's Bluetooth adverts fill in 1 % levels when you open it nearby.
- Listening modes, press-and-hold cycle, Adaptive level, Noise Cancellation with one AirPod,
  conversation awareness, personalized volume.
- Ear detection: pause when you take an AirPod out, play again when it goes back, pause when you fall asleep.
- Stem presses with your own actions, including Overview and Show desktop.
- Head gestures: live Yes/No demo, calibration, "anytime" actions, nod to answer calls on phones.
- Press speed, hold duration, volume swipe, call controls, microphone side, tone volume, case
  sounds, custom EQ, optimized charging, rename, model and firmware.
