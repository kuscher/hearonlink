HearOn Link brings your AirPods' settings to Android laptops and phones. It talks to the AirPods over their own control channel, the way an iPhone or Mac does, so you can see each part's battery, switch noise control and choose what a stem press does. No root, no account, no internet.

Battery for each part
Left, right and case, with charging, in the app, the Quick Settings tile and panel, a quiet notification and a home-screen widget. When a part stops reporting, HearOn Link shows its last level and how long ago it was seen; the case's Bluetooth adverts fill in exact levels when you open it nearby.

Listening modes
Off, Transparency, Adaptive and Noise Cancellation in one tap, which modes a press-and-hold switches between, and Noise Cancellation with one AirPod. Conversation awareness and personalized volume too, on AirPods that have them.

Ear detection
Pause when you take an AirPod out and play again when it goes back in, or pause when you fall asleep.

Stem presses your way
Press once, twice, three times or hold, for both buds or each one: play or pause, next, previous, noise control, volume, voice assistant, or a system action such as Overview or Show desktop. Presses left on "AirPods default" keep working the same with your other devices.

Head gestures
Try them in a live demo that answers Yes or No, calibrate them to your own head, and map a nod and a shake to actions. On phones, nod to accept a call and shake your head to decline.

More settings
Press speed and hold duration, volume swipe, call controls, microphone side, tone volume, case sounds, custom EQ, optimized charging, renaming, and model and firmware details. Heart rate and hearing protection on AirPods Pro 3. HearOn Link only shows what your AirPods report they support.

Made for laptops
One window with your AirPods on the left and settings on the right, a Quick Settings tile with a small panel, and it starts on its own when the AirPods connect, after you link them once in Android's device picker.

Why HearOn Link can use an accessibility service
Show desktop, Overview, Back, Notifications, Quick Settings, Screenshot and Lock need HearOn Link's optional system-actions service. It only performs the actions you pick for a stem press or a head gesture: it receives no accessibility events, can't read the screen and doesn't see what you type or tap. It stays off until you turn it on in Android's settings.

Private
No internet permission, no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Your AirPods' settings stay on your device.

Requirements
Bluetooth AirPods or Beats with an Apple chip. On phones, Android 17, or Android 16 QPR3 on a Pixel: older Bluetooth stacks can't open the AirPods' control channel. Some features need an Apple device and aren't included: Find My, spatial audio, Siri, audio sharing and firmware updates.
